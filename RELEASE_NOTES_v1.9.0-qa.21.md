Visual-only continuation of the Character sheet redesign.

## Inventory & Gear
- Tokens of Power, Containers, Equipment frame and Unassigned Gear now sit on parchment panels.

- Equipment Figure controls move onto a paper control surface.
- The central Equipment paper-doll stage and equipment slots intentionally remain dark.
  This preserves the approved Gear target design and keeps the body/slot workspace as the focal interactive area.
- Slot Guide becomes a light parchment tray.
